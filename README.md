One original one-line horror story, written by `main.py` to `entry.txt` for make.fun's "One Line, One Chill" bounty.

> Every night the footsteps in the attic spell out my name, one letter at a time, and tonight they started on yours.

114 characters, one sentence. The footsteps spelling a name is concrete and vivid, the slow nightly pace builds dread, and the final turn hands the scare to the reader — tonight it is not my name being spelled any more, it is yours. Nothing is shown and nothing is explained; the reader fills in the worst of it.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/chill-horror-line-out python3 main.py
```
